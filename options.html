Minerr

Underlines Japanese words by how well you know them, shows definitions on hover, and adds Anki cards.

Dictionaries

    Anki

    Anki makes the deck if it does not exist.

    Lapis is the note type of the mining guide. Minerr fills its fields and never changes it. Minerr makes the classic note type and keeps it up to date. If you select your own note type, Minerr only fills its fields and never changes the note type.

    Anki has no Lapis note type. Minerr downloads Lapis.apkg from the Lapis project (GPL-3.0) and imports it into Anki.

    Fields

    Write the content of each field. Minerr replaces these markers when it adds a card. The other text stays as you write it, and it can contain HTML.

    {word}
    The word in dictionary form
    {reading}
    The reading in kana
    {furigana}
    The word with its reading for Anki furigana, 食べる[たべる]
    {sentence}
    The sentence, with the word in bold
    {definition}
    The definitions with their tags
    {image}
    The screenshot of the tab
    {audio}
    The audio of the subtitle line, for a word in video subtitles
    {wordaudio}
    The audio of the word from JapanesePod101
    {url}
    The address of the page
    {title}
    The title of the page

    Known words

    Mark the words of cards that you already know in Anki as known.

    The words of the cards that match this search become known. The default search finds the cards in the default deck with an interval of 21 days or more.

    The note field that holds the word in dictionary form.

    Word statuses

    Save the statuses of your words to a file, or load them from a file. An import keeps your other words, and the status in the file wins. Unknown words are not in the file.

      Colors and shortcuts

      Stop the mouse on a word, or hold Shift on it, to open its popup. The status keys, A, and E work while the popup is open. With text selected, a status key marks every word in the selection. No key works in a text field.

      KeyActionOn a page
      AAdd an Anki card
      EEdit the card, then send it to Anki
      EscClose the popup
      Alt+SLook up the selected text
      ShiftOn a word in a popup: open a popup over it (up to 4)
      NOpen the popup of the next unknown word of the subtitle line
      Alt+Shift+DTurn Minerr off or on for this site
      Alt+Shift+STurn the sentence highlight on or off
      PageDown Space ↓Reader: next page
      PageUp Shift+Space ↑Reader: previous page
      → ←Reader: next and previous page. In vertical text, ← is the next page.

      Video keys

      These keys work on a video with the Minerr subtitle overlay: on YouTube, in the video player, and on other sites. They do not work in a text field. On YouTube, Minerr takes a key only while the overlay shows subtitles. Some keys need subtitles with times, for example from WhisperX or a subtitle file.

      KeyActionNote

      Video

      The subtitle overlay on YouTube, in the video player, and on other sites.

      A card from a subtitle line gets the audio of the line, and this much audio before and after it.

      Play mode

      An i+1 line has exactly one unknown word. The play mode needs subtitles with times.

      Play again after a pause of the play mode

      The video also stays paused while the popup of a subtitle word is open. It plays again when the mouse goes away and the popup closes.

      The text shows when you move the mouse onto it or pause the video. Press W on a video to turn this on or off.

      Second subtitles

      A second subtitle track, for example English, shows under the line. On YouTube, Minerr gets the English subtitles from the server. Press Shift+W on a video to show or hide them.

      Removes text in brackets, for example （笑） or [音楽], from the lines. A line with only bracketed text does not show.

      Parts with no subtitles

      For a part of 1.5 seconds or more with no line. The last 0.5 seconds before the next line play at the normal speed. Needs subtitles with times.

      Word popup

      Choose when the word popup opens. Hover opens it when the mouse stops on a word. Hover on unknown words opens it only on unknown words. Hold Shift to open the popup at once in all modes.

      Furigana

      Show the reading in hiragana over the words with kanji. Unknown words shows it only over words with a red underline. On hover shows it over the word under the mouse.

      Pitch colors

      Color the words on a page by their pitch accent pattern. Minerr needs a pitch accent dictionary for this. Words that are not in the dictionary keep their color.

        Theme

        The theme of this page, the toolbar menu, and the word popup. System follows the setting of your computer. Anki cards follow the night mode of Anki.
